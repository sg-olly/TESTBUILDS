What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. Along with similar technologies like local storage, they help the site work properly, remember your choices, and give us a sense of how the site is being used so we can improve it.

How we use cookies

We keep cookies to a minimum and only use them to run the site and understand its performance, never to build advertising profiles or sell your data.

The cookies we use

TypePurpose
EssentialRemember your cookie choice (stored in your browser's local storage) and keep the site secure and functioning. These can't be switched off.
AnalyticsOnly set if you accept them. Help us understand, in an anonymised and aggregated way, how visitors use the site so we can improve it.
Embedded servicesSet by third-party features such as the Typeform enquiry form, needed for those features to work.

Third-party services

Some features rely on trusted external providers that may set their own cookies or see your IP address when your browser requests their files:

  • Typeform: powers our project enquiry form.
  • Fontshare: serves the typeface used across the site.
  • jsDelivr: serves the small script behind the site's smooth scrolling.

These providers have their own privacy and cookie policies governing how they handle data.

Managing your cookies

When you first arrive you can accept or decline non-essential cookies using the banner at the bottom of the screen. You can change your mind at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings, which will bring the banner back.

Most browsers also let you block or delete cookies entirely through their settings. Please note that blocking essential cookies may affect how parts of the site work.
