Who we are

Studio Green ("we", "us", "our") is an independent marketing and web design studio operating as studiogreenmarketing.co.uk. We're the data controller responsible for any personal data you share with us through this website.

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your data, email us at hello@studiogreenmarketing.co.uk.

What we collect

We only collect what we need to respond to you and run the site:

  • Details you give us: your name, email address, and anything you tell us when you submit the project enquiry form or email us directly.
  • Usage data: basic, anonymised information about how the site is used (pages visited, device and browser type), collected through cookies and analytics if you accept them.

We don't knowingly collect special category data and we don't buy or sell personal information.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss a potential project.
  • To provide and improve our services if we work together.
  • To understand how the site is performing and make it better.
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations.

We don't use your data for automated decision-making or profiling.

Our legal basis

Under UK GDPR we rely on:

  • Consent: when you submit the enquiry form or accept non-essential cookies.
  • Legitimate interests: to respond to enquiries and keep the site secure and working well.
  • Legal obligation: where we're required to keep records.

Sharing and third parties

We keep your data to ourselves wherever possible. We use a small number of trusted providers to run the site:

  • Typeform: powers the project enquiry form and processes the details you submit.
  • Hosting and email: our website host and email provider store the site and our correspondence.
  • Content delivery: Fontshare serves our typeface and jsDelivr serves a small scrolling script; both may see your IP address when your browser requests those files.
  • Analytics: if you accept analytics cookies, an analytics provider helps us understand site usage in an aggregated, anonymised form.

These providers only process your data on our instructions or as needed to deliver their service. See our cookie policy for more on cookies and embedded services.

Where your data lives

Some of the providers we use may store or process data outside the UK. Where that happens, we make sure it's protected by recognised safeguards, such as a UK adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses, so it gets the same level of protection as it would here.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry data for as long as needed to respond and, if we work together, for the duration of the project plus any period required for legal and accounting purposes. After that it's securely deleted.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Receive a copy of it in a portable format.
  • Object to or restrict how we use it.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • Complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

To exercise any of these, just email hello@studiogreenmarketing.co.uk.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we work hard to keep your information safe.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date.

Questions about your privacy or this policy? Email hello@studiogreenmarketing.co.uk and we'll get back to you within one working day.
